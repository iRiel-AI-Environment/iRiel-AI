About iRiel

iRiel AI Environment is the central development identity for the iRiel AI ecosystem, an initiative by Robert Software Labs to make sophisticated artificial intelligence feel simple, accessible, and trustworthy.

We envision a unified personal intelligence environment where people can think, communicate, research, create, organise, and accomplish tasks without navigating a fragmented collection of AI products and interfaces.

We believe technology should adapt to people — not the other way around.

What we’re building

• iRiel AI — A human-centred personal intelligence experience for everyday work, knowledge, and creativity.
• iRiel Intelligence Core — The envisioned coordination layer for context, model providers, capabilities, and execution.
• PRSL-AI Build Intelligence — AI-assisted software engineering, application building, and product development capabilities.
• iRiel Writing Intelligence — Writing, language, and documentation assistance grounded in accessibility and user intent.
• Agent & Developer Infrastructure — Composable tools, typed integrations, SDKs, and inspectable agent workflows.

These are areas of development and product direction; individual repositories will document their own implementation status and availability.

Our principles

|Principle                  |Commitment                                                                    |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|**Human First**            |Make intelligence understandable, inclusive, and useful.                      |
|**Privacy Second**         |Build around consent, data minimisation, and user control.                    |
|**Technology Last**        |Keep infrastructure complexity behind calm, intuitive experiences.            |
|**Accessibility by design**|Treat accessible interactions as a foundational requirement.                  |
|**Reliable engineering**   |Prefer clear contracts, explicit execution, testing, and observable behaviour.|
|**Interoperability**       |Design modular systems with deliberate integration boundaries.                |

Engineering approach

Our development philosophy favours TypeScript, strong typing, schema-validated inputs, deterministic application logic, event-driven and streaming workflows, structured error handling, and test-driven development.

We value KISS, DRY, clear documentation, composability, security-conscious defaults, and production-quality user experiences.
